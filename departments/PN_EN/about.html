About Us

The Department of Pediatric Nursing is responsible for all academic endeavors leading to the excellence in the care of children and family.

Vision

To be a leader in pediatric nursing research and academics in Thailand and Southeast Asia by 2011.

Mission
  1. Offer pediatric nursing courses to students in baccalaureate and master's programs
  2. Conduct research studies in pediatric nursing and apply their findings in pediatric nursing practice.
  3. Provide academic services in pediatric nursing to various groups
  4. Support and participate in Thai cultural activities
  5. Develop effective administrative management system
  6. Initiate collaborative research and academic networking in pediatric nursing with international institutions
