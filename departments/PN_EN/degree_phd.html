Doctor of Philosophy Program in Nursing Science

The program of study emphasizes research skills and in-depth knowledge in nursing science so that the graduates will demonstrate competence in all phases of research methods and conduct research that is grounded in, and in turn informs, theory, practice, and policy.

Doctor of Nursing Science Program (D.N.S.)

Nurses are leading health professionals, acting as operatives and researchers playing an important role in the development of people's health. They find problems and solutions to health challenges for people in both at-risk groups and groups with health problems in emergency and chronic crises. Our program prepares them to synthesize knowledge, develop research leading to innovation in nursing and health service systems that respond to changing situations affecting health conditions both at the individual, family and social level, according to moral, ethical and professional standards perfectly and holistically.
